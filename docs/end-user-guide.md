# TADPODS End-User Guide

## Signing in

Open the TADPODS web address, enter your email and password, and select **Sign in to TADPODS**. Sessions refresh securely while you work. Use **Sign out** on shared devices.

## Navigation

The left navigation contains Dashboard, Sales, Purchasing, Inventory, Customers, Suppliers, Reports and Administration. TADPODS shows only actions allowed by your role. Modules scheduled for the next build phase are visibly unavailable rather than opening dead screens.

## Search and actions

Press `Ctrl+K` or select **Search and actions**. Type the action you need, then open the matching destination. Results follow your permissions.

## Dashboard

Every dashboard value links to the records behind it. Red status indicators require attention. Green indicators confirm that the platform foundation is operating normally.

## Getting access changed

Ask a TADPODS administrator to change your role. Do not share accounts or passwords. Role changes appear in the audit history.
